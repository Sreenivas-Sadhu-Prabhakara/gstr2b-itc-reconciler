How the matching works

      
An invoice is the same invoice on both sides when three things agree: the supplier's GSTIN, the invoice number and the financial year of the invoice date.

      
Invoice numbers are compared after being reduced to a common form, because the supplier's spelling and yours are rarely identical. The number is upper-cased, spaces and the separators / - \ . are removed, and the leading zeros of each run of digits are dropped — so inv-001/23, INV1/23 and Inv 001 / 23 are all read as the same number.

      
The financial year is part of the key because invoice numbering restarts every 1 April: INV-1 of 2025-26 is a different document from INV-1 of 2024-25. The Indian financial year runs 1 April to 31 March, so an invoice dated 31 March 2026 belongs to 2025-26 and one dated 1 April 2026 to 2026-27.

      
For invoices found on both sides, the taxable value and each of IGST, CGST, SGST and cess are compared separately. A head that differs by more than the tolerance is reported as a value mismatch, with the difference for each head.

      
Anything in your books but not in GSTR-2B is the number that matters. Input tax credit can only be taken on an invoice that appears in GSTR-2B, so the tax on those invoices is cash you cannot claim yet. They are totalled as the ITC at risk and grouped by supplier, largest amount first, so you know whom to chase.


      

        What is compared, and what is not
        
    
          
  • The B2B section of GSTR-2B (docdata.b2b) is reconciled. Amendments, credit and debit notes, imports and ISD credit are reported as present but not reconciled — check those by hand.
    • 
          
  • The comparison tolerance is ₹1.00 per tax head by default, to absorb line-level rounding. Set it to zero to demand the exact paisa.
    • 
          
  • All amounts are held in whole paise, so the totals are exact.
    • 
          
  • A missing figure in either file counts as zero. A missing supplier GSTIN, invoice number or invoice date is reported as an error, because an invoice cannot be matched without all three.
    • 
          
  • Supplier GSTINs in the purchase register are checked for the standard 15-character shape only, not for the check character.
    • 
          
  • Where the same invoice appears twice in your register, the first copy is compared and the rest are listed as duplicates, so nothing is counted twice.
    • 
        

      


      
This tool compares two files you give it. It cannot tell you whether a credit is eligible: eligibility also depends on receipt of the goods or services, payment of tax by the supplier, the time limit for claiming, blocked credits, and reversal rules. Confirm the current law and your own figures with the GST portal and a qualified practitioner before filing GSTR-3B.
